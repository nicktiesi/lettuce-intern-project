House 71

Where American classics meet modern craft. A contemporary tavern inspired by the country's diverse culinary traditions, blending timeless hospitality with a vibrant social atmosphere.

American, Reimagined

House 71 draws its menu from regional American classics — Maryland crab, Philly cheesesteaks, Cajun shrimp & grits, Chicago Italian beef — served in a warm, wood-toned dining room built for a bustling bar scene and easy, all-day hospitality.

The Space

House 71 takes over a corner restaurant space in the newly renovated, historic Bowen Building at 15th & Eye Streets NW — the heart of McPherson Square and DC's East End, steps from the White House, the U.S. Treasury Department, and McPherson Square Metro. The corner exposure carries patio opportunity along 15th Street, with neighbors like Compass Coffee, Taylor Gourmet, &pizza, and Bobby Van's nearby.
