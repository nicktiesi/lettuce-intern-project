Our Story

House 71 is a contemporary American tavern inspired by the country's diverse culinary traditions, blending timeless hospitality with a vibrant social atmosphere. The space feels sophisticated but welcoming, with a bustling bar, warm design elements, and a menu that offers select American classics from each region of the country and their cultures.

Our kitchen brings that regional spirit to life through a menu of craveable, elevated plates and sandwiches — built with the same attention to detail, whether it's a lively lunch, a long dinner, or a casual reception.

The Space

House 71 takes the corner restaurant space in the newly renovated, historic Bowen Building at 15th and Eye Streets NW — a McPherson Square property neighboring the White House and home to JPMorgan Chase's regional HQ.

The corner exposure carries 32'6" of frontage and a patio opportunity along 15th Street, under 20-foot ceilings, between high office density and steady tourist traffic.
